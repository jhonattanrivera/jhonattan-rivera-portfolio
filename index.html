Step 1 of 5

Someone taps a card on a terminal or confirms a purchase online. For them, this is where it starts and where it ends.

Step 2 of 5

The gateway receives the card data, tokenizes it so nobody handles the real number, and decides where to route the payment.

I worked here at PayU and Kushki.

Step 3 of 5

The acquirer is the merchant's bank or processor. It builds the ISO 8583 message and hands it to the right network.

I lead integrations here, at Akua.

Step 4 of 5

Visa, Mastercard or a local network carries the message to the bank that issued the card, applying its own rules along the way.

Step 5 of 5

The issuer checks balance, risk and authentication, then answers: approved or declined.

I worked here at Pomelo.

00 Approved
